Software Requirements Specification

Online Banking Management System

Functional Requirements

FR-001: The customer shall register using an email address.

FR-002: The customer shall login using email and password.

FR-003: The customer shall transfer money to another account.

FR-004: The customer shall view transaction history.

Non-Functional Requirements

NFR-001: The system shall respond to user requests within 2 seconds.

NFR-002: The system shall encrypt customer passwords.
